## Anchor evidence already found
- Sounds, 9 Nov 1974, "The cost of rock" (Geoff Barton), reprinted at geirmykl.wordpress.com:
  ticket prices: 50p cheap gig, ~£2 average for a reasonably big band, £3.50 Wembley supergig;
  10cc fee ~£1,000/gig with ~£750 of costs before the band earns; band has 6 road crew, 3 vehicles;
  Gallagher & Lyle self-financed tour: £15,000 outlay, ~£3,000 back; 29 people on the road; hotel £3,500;
  orchestra wages £5,000; lorry + coach £1,500; they charged max £1/ticket; VAT quoted at 8%.
  (All figures 1974 GBP; these are mid-size acts, NOT beginners — flag accordingly when reused.)
  URL: https://geirmykl.wordpress.com/2019/04/19/article-about-the-cost-of-rock-from-sounds-november-9-1974/
- Wikipedia: pub rock scene 1972-75, Stiff Records formed from a £400 loan; pub-rock label break-even
  ~2,000 copies vs ~20,000 for mainstream (Laing, One Chord Wonders, cited). Needs second source.
  URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pub_rock_(United_Kingdom)
